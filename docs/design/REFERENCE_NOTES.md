## What the references say together
1. **Playful + hopeful, not minimal.** Cute flat characters with dot eyes (03, 02, 09), grainy warm figures (24), jokes in copy (15). The previous redesign was too sterile.
2. **Grain everywhere it matters.** Airbrushed/stippled gradients with film grain (10, 18, 24, 25, 32). A fine grain overlay unifies glass and paint.
3. **Flowers and growth.** Soft 3D flora (16, 17), generative blooms (28), translucent watercolour-glass petals (29), dandelions (25). Growth is the metaphor for learning.
4. **Hands holding something precious** (25, 26, 31, 32) — a gentle, human illustration motif.
5. **Cards, stacks, decks, collages** (05, 06, 11, 12, 13, 14, 30, 33, 34), tilted, overlapping, with things peeking out.
6. **Type with personality.** Giant serif words (08, 13, 30), blurred display type (05, 12, 14), chunky grotesque (03), Swiss lowercase (22, 23), pixel serif (18). Each surface can have its own voice.
7. **Blue as home.** Sky/cobalt/ultramarine recurs (01, 02, 06, 22, 23, 30) next to soft pastels and warm off-white.
8. **Glass where it helps:** pills, nav, folders, orbs (05, 06, 11, 12, 31, 34).
9. **Great motion:** scene choreography (03), drawing stems + popping petals (28), blooming (29), deck shuffles (33), crossfading AI lines + orb (34).
