Each reference was studied on its own. Numbering follows the order in the issue (r01..r34).
r05 and r27 are the same file (posted twice).

User's words in the issue: "a little playful and hopeful. I don't want the app to look too serious and
too minimal. I also don't want those productivity to-do list type AI slop."

# Synthesis: what the user's vision actually is

1. PLAYFUL + HOPEFUL, not serious and minimal. Characters with eyes (r03, r02, r04, r09, r24), bright
   saturated accents (cobalt, coral, butter, magenta, lime), confetti, pop-in motion.
2. SOFT SKY GLASS remains the base (r01, r05, r12, r14): sky-blue / periwinkle / lilac gradients, frosted
   cards, white canvas. The previous redesign got this part right; it lacked character and depth.
3. STACKED / TILTED / FANNED CARDS everywhere (r04, r05, r06, r07, r12, r13, r14, r30, r31, r32). This is
   the single most repeated pattern. Today should be a collage/deck, not a list.
4. GRAIN + GRADIENT ILLUSTRATION (r10, r18, r24, r25, r34) and PAINTERLY backgrounds (r19) for
   share cards and vocab.
5. GROWTH motif: flowers, sprouts, petals, seeds (r16, r17, r25, r26, r28, r29). Motion = stems draw,
   petals pop.
6. Typography with personality: a heavy display face for big moments (r03), elegant serif for words
   and quotes (r08, r10, r13, r30), grotesk for staggered posters (r22), pixel accents (r18, r05).
7. TIME CAPSULE = glass folders with peeking letters, labelled by date, on a cobalt-to-night gradient
   (r06, r11, r31), large fading date title, 3D lift-and-flip to open.
8. ONBOARDING = Abode-style morphing characters + clouds (r03), huge dashed-to-solid interest pills
   (r15), "getting to know you" aurora + orb greeting (r32).
9. Mood = shape characters with faces (r09, r04).
10. Logo: keep the eight-ray mark the user likes, but make the icon meaningful: rays become petals
    (r17), or an hourglass whose neck holds a seed/heart (r33). Kairos = "the right moment".
