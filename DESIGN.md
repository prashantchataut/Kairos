# Kairos — a little room to grow

A hopeful pocket garden for learning, reflection, memories and play. The visual
signature is a small painted seed companion beneath clear glass controls.

- Pond blue `#245F9E`, leaf green `#34765E`, rose `#AC5068`.
- Pale sky `#F0F7F8`, paper `#F9FCFB`, deep ink `#18353F`.
- Night pond surfaces for dark mode; semantic colors follow the active theme.
- Rounded Android sans for navigation and headlines; bundled Lora for thoughts
  and letters. Typography comes from the Material scale.
- 24dp screen padding, 8/12/16/24 spacing, 24–28dp reading-object corners.
- Glass belongs to navigation, small doors and folded-letter surfaces. Long text
  stays on an opaque or high-contrast surface.
- Native Canvas artwork uses translucent pigment pools, a pond, flowers and a
  seed companion. No image downloads or paid API are required at runtime.
- User-triggered flower growth and page state transitions are short and honor
  reduced motion. No permanent celebratory confetti or punitive streak effects.
- Short labels, flexible rows, scrollable content and 48dp minimum touch controls.
- On wide windows, content stays readable within 720dp and navigation uses a rail.

## Main places

Today: painted garden, capsule/play doors, daily word, a thought and a writing
invitation. Learn: a garden session card and searchable words. Reflect: one
question and a chronological memory shelf. Library: readable quotations,
proverbs, idioms and phrases with saved/search/share. Capsules: folded-letter
archive, private editor, deliberate reveal and linked replies. Play: an original
word garden plus explicitly sourced external adventures.

## Identity

The seed mark is a white seed containing a blue moment. It shares geometry in
Compose, adaptive launcher foreground, monochrome icon and splash. Its readable
silhouette matters more than effects at launcher size.

See `docs/rebuild/REVIEW.md` for measurements, preserved infrastructure, actual
validation and explicit limits. Device screenshots and frame timing are still
