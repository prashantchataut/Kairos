# Kairos launch film: treatment

**Logline (12 words):** Most days just pass; Kairos keeps the one moment that should stay.

## Specifics first

- **The name.** καιρός is "the right moment", the opposite of χρόνος, clock time. The app's mark
  already says this: eight petals, one of them (one o'clock) the "moment" petal, like a clock hand.
- **Unlike the obvious version** (a daily-quote app with a fixed list), Kairos *learns*: what you keep,
  share and linger on decides the next word, thought or book in the Feed; your own words come back as
  flashcards; and a letter you write today opens months from now.
- **Its own objects:** the word card deck (Today), the reel cards (Feed), flashcards (Play), the letter
  and its envelope (time capsule), share cards in pin / story / square, Kai, the bloom.

## Three concepts

1. **Clock hand becomes a petal** (lens c, formal device): a clock face spinning through days; one hand
   stops and turns coral; that coral petal is carried through every product shot and finally becomes
   the moment petal of the bloom. *Chosen*: the device *is* the name's meaning and the mark's meaning.
2. **A year of skipped mornings** (lens b, pain): a calendar strip of grey days flicks by; one day lights
   up. Rejected: calendars are every habit app's prop.
3. **Words that drift into a sky** (lens a, verb): words float up like clouds and are caught. Rejected:
   reads as a weather app, and floating text is hard to read.

**Specificity test.** Would the film be as true of a plain quote-of-the-day app? No: the Feed beat
shows a card kept and the next cards changing to match ("It learns / what you keep."), Play shows the
user's *own* kept words coming back, and the letter beat shows a date months away.
**Feature-word test.** Muted, a stranger would say "word of the day", "feed", "flashcards", "letter
to my future self", "share card". Each is shown in its real app form.
**Deletion test.** Without the demo beats, "Days just pass / Some moments stay / Kairos, the right
moment" still reads; without the value beats it would be a feature tour, so they stay.
