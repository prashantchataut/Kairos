# Kairos — Product Context

## Register
product (Android, Jetpack Compose)

## What Kairos is
**A small, hopeful corner of your phone for spending the day on things that make you feel good.**
One word to learn, one thought to keep, a letter to your future self, and a few gentle games — with
a little watercolor friend named Pip keeping you company.

Tagline used in-product: **"Make today a little lovelier."**

## Who it is for
The maker and their community: people who open their phone between other things and want a
few minutes that leave them feeling lighter, not busier. Ages ~16–35, comfortable with playful
UI, allergic to productivity guilt.

## Core loops
1. **Today** — say how you feel (Pip mirrors you), read today's word and today's thought,
   pick up one small joy, peek at your next capsule.
2. **Learn** — a curated vocabulary + quote engine. Daily picks are deterministic per date,
   shaped by what you save. Practice uses spaced repetition (SM-2) with flashcards and quizzes.
3. **Capsule** — write a letter to future-you, attach photos, files or a voice note, seal it
   until a date. It stays locked (really locked) until then, then you get a notification and
   an opening moment.
4. **Play** — Kairos Originals (built-in mini games made for this app) plus a downloadable
   community library of Pokémon fan games, driven by a catalog you can extend.

## Principles
- **Playful and hopeful, never serious-minimal.** Colour, a mascot, and soft motion are part
  of the product, not decoration on top of it.
- **Not a to-do app.** No checklists, no task inboxes, no guilt streaks. Progress is shown as
  a jar that fills with joy petals and words that bloom — never a red number.
- **Local-first.** Everything works offline. Network is only used to download games and a
  remote game catalog the user chooses.
- **Honest.** No fake community activity. Game entries link to their real authors.
- **One obvious action per screen.**

## Voice
Warm, short, a little funny. Plain sentences. "Sealed until 12 March 2027." "Pip is proud of
you." No "unlock your potential", no therapy-speak, no "journey".

## Anti-references
Crowded habit dashboards, guilt streaks, to-do lists dressed as self-care, purple AI gradients,
cream-and-terracotta "premium" templates, glass on every surface without hierarchy.
