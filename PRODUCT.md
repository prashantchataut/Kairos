# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Users
People who want a small, good daily ritual: a word worth knowing, a thought worth keeping, one small kind thing to do, and a place to write to their future self. Opened between other things, morning or evening, on a phone. The owner (Prashant) builds it for himself and his community. They should leave feeling lighter and hopeful, never busier.

## Product Purpose
Kairos (Greek: the right moment) helps someone spend a few minutes a day on something that makes them feel good: learning one lovely word, keeping one thought, doing one small good thing, and sealing letters, photos and voice notes to open later. Success is a person who returns because it feels good, not because a streak nags them.

## Positioning
Every word, quote, small good thing and capsule prompt is written for one person by an AI curator that learns from what they save, skip, find too easy or too hard, forget in practice, and how they feel. Nothing is bundled. The time capsule and shareable art cards turn those moments into keepsakes.

## Operating Context
Android phones (min SDK 26). Each person brings their own OpenRouter key (stored encrypted; never bundled). Works offline from a small pool the curator keeps ready. Notifications: a daily nudge at a chosen time; a letter-is-ready alert. Cards are shared to Instagram/Pinterest-style feeds and saved to the gallery.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Today (word, thought, mood check-in, small good thing, review, capsule), Learn (library board, "ask for words about…", practice with SM-2), word and quote pages, card studio (share/save 1080px), time capsule (compose with photos/videos/files/voice; seal; countdown; unlock; reply), You (activity, moods, settings: curator key/model, interests and level, reminder, motion, theme), onboarding. Local-first: Room + DataStore, no account, no ads, no analytics. Letters never leave the phone.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Kairos. Personality: **playful and hopeful**, warm and grown-up at once; never "too serious and too minimal", never productivity/to-do-list AI slop.
- Visual references in issue #606 are binding and take priority over any generic taste rule. See `docs/design/REFERENCE_NOTES.md`.
- Owner asked for: liquid glass + cute 2D + acrylic watercolour; great (not only subtle) animation everywhere — onboarding, saving, adding the API key; an adaptive slider; a logo with personal meaning (keep the eight-ray mark, replace the poor launcher icon).
- Voice: warm, specific, a little witty; plain sentences; no mystical self-help clichés, no exclamation-mark spam.

## Evidence on Hand
No user testimonials, metrics or press exist; none may be invented. No photography is bundled; imagery is drawn in code (flowers, characters, textures) or comes from the person's own capsule attachments. Content is AI-generated at runtime.

## Product Principles
1. One good moment beats a dashboard: each screen has one thing that matters most.
2. Delight is earned by care: big moments (first launch, saving, sealing, connecting the curator, blooming) get real choreography; frequent actions stay quick.
3. The curator explains itself and listens: every pick has a reason; every reaction teaches it.
4. Kind by default: no streak guilt, no fake urgency, gentle copy for low moods.
5. Private and local-first: the person owns everything; AI is opt-in with their own key.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Text contrast ≥4.5:1 on every surface (including on gradients and glass); 48dp touch targets; a "Calmer motion" setting plus the system animator scale reduce travel and loops while keeping fades; TalkBack labels on every control.
